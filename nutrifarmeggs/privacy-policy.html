Privacy Policy

Nutri Farm Eggs

At Nutri Farm Eggs, we respect your privacy. We do not collect or store personal information from visitors to this website. There are no accounts, sign-ups or forms to fill in.

We only store your name, phone number and delivery address when you place an order with us by phone or WhatsApp. We use this information only to process and deliver your order. If you want your details deleted, contact us on +91 83109 01383 or +91 97387 67889, or email nutrifarmeggs@gmail.com, and we will promptly remove them from our records.

Orders and cancellations are handled directly through phone calls or WhatsApp messages to our contact numbers.

We do not use cookies, analytics or tracking technologies on this website. For its fonts, the site loads from Google Fonts, so Google may receive your IP address when the page loads. Clicking a WhatsApp, call or email button opens that app, and its own privacy policy applies from there.

Your privacy and trust are important to us. If you have any questions about privacy, please contact us at the numbers above.

This privacy policy will be reviewed periodically and updated as needed.
