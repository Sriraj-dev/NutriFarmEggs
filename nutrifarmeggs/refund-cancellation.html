Refund & Cancellation Policy

Organic Nutri Farm

At Organic Nutri Farm, we want you to be happy with every tray of eggs. This policy explains how cancellations and refunds work.

Order Cancellation

You can cancel an order before it is dispatched for delivery. To cancel, call or message us on WhatsApp at the numbers below. We do not accept cancellations through any other means. Once an order is out for delivery, it can no longer be cancelled.

Damaged or Broken Eggs

Eggs are fragile. If any eggs arrive broken or damaged, please contact us on the same day of delivery with a photo of the tray, and we will replace the affected eggs or refund their value.

Wrong or Unsatisfactory Order

If you receive a different product or tray size than you ordered, or you are not satisfied with the quality, tell us on the day of delivery. We will review the issue and arrange a replacement or refund.

Non-Returnable Items

Because eggs are perishable food, we cannot accept returns of eggs once delivered, except in the cases above.

How Refunds Are Paid

Approved refunds are returned using the same method you paid with, or as agreed with you over the phone. Payment and refund details are confirmed with you when you place your order.
