Shipping & Delivery Policy

Organic Nutri Farm

This policy explains how we deliver your eggs from Organic Nutri Farm.

Delivery Area

We deliver only within the local areas we serve. Please confirm with us by phone or WhatsApp whether we can deliver to your location. Bulk orders for shops and restaurants can be arranged on request.

Placing an Order

Orders are placed by calling or messaging us on WhatsApp. We will confirm your products, quantity, price and delivery address with you.

Delivery Time

Delivery time is confirmed when you place your order. We strive to deliver on time, but we are not responsible for delays caused by circumstances beyond our control, such as weather or traffic.

Delivery Charges

Any delivery charges, and any minimum order for free delivery, will be told to you when you place your order, before you confirm.

Packaging and Handling

Eggs are packed in trays and handled with care to reduce breakage. Please check your order at the time of delivery and tell us right away about any problem.

Delivery Address and Availability

Please give a complete address and be reachable at the phone number you ordered with. If we cannot reach you or deliver after reasonable attempts, the order may be cancelled.
